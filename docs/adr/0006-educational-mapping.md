# 0006 — Educational NIST and EU mapping

## Status

Accepted (2026-10-17).

## Context

Readers will ask how the tier rubric relates to the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. Mapping them without a disclaimer would over-claim.

## Decision

`docs/RISKS.md` maps each implemented control to a NIST function and warns which EU AI Act bucket it is easy to confuse with our internal "high". The HTML banner repeats that the page is not a certification and not legal advice.

## Consequences

We do not say "compliant". We do not classify the fictional agents into EU risk classes. A real engagement would need counsel; that is out of scope.
